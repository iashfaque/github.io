Crisis & Incident Response

Containing a Malicious Insider

An insider altering customer data across 81 accounts.
01

The Challenge

A customer-service agent was modifying customer email addresses without justification — a live data-integrity and trust risk carrying legal and regulatory exposure.

The Diagnosis

Investigation from the first alert identified 81 affected accounts, plus two modifications driven by social engineering. Early response had no clear command structure.

The Intervention

Drove containment from the alert stage, brought stakeholders up to speed, assigned tasks across teams, and stepped in as second-in-command — coordinating with HR and Business Conduct & Ethics.

Automation Reliability · CoE

Fixing the Automation Behind the SOC

An out-of-order rule defect that cost the team hundreds of hours.
05

The Challenge

A change to the SOC’s self-service automation (AutoSIM) caused rules to run out of order, blocking incident responders from triage tickets and slowing response across the queue.

The Diagnosis

The defect carried a potential impact of up to ~455 work hours; rule execution order was never enforced, so the failure kept recurring.

The Intervention

Led a Center-of-Excellence deep-dive with the automation team, corrected every existing rule, and applied tagging to enforce a sorted execution order so the failure could not return.
